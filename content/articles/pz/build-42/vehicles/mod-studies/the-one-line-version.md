# The one-line version

> Source: 24-synthesis-and-assessment.md (compiled 2026-08-10, verified against Project Zomboid 42.20). Imported 2026-09-29. Confidence tags in the text are the original author's.

Our spine is better than anyone's and we have almost no product on top of it. The
fastest route to a mod worth playing is to stop building spine, fix the two
correctness defects the crash test exposed, settle the multiplayer contract
before it is baked in wrong, and then spend everything on actions and
consequence -- starting with the muffler, because vanilla already built most of
it and nobody noticed.
