# The headline, corrected

> Source: 17-vehicle-animation-reference.md (compiled 2026-08-09, verified against Project Zomboid 42.20). Imported 2026-09-29. Confidence tags in the text are the original author's.

B42 supports animated vehicle parts natively. That much was right.

What the earlier document got wrong is the shape of the work. It said this was
"an art problem plus about twenty lines of script". In practice the twenty lines
were easy and **the art was never the hard part either** -- the expensive part
was a chain of failure modes that produce identical symptoms and log nothing.

Budget accordingly. The mechanism is a day. The silent-failure surface is the
project.
